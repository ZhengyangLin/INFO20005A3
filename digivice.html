PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Introducing the 25th anniversary version of Digivice with color LCDs!
Rechargeable battery, LED and vibration motor inside.

While following the play of the original products, a variety of effects are now available in color. Super power up!
Choose your favorite child and follow the story to explore the digital world.
You can adventure with your partner Digimon while listening to the opening song Butterfly.
The boss Digimon battles are reproduced with LEDs and vibrations.
Like the SCSA series, it also includes a mode to enjoy background music and famous lines.
A newly-drawn Digica, commemorating the 25th anniversary, is also included as a purchase bonus.

* This product uses Japanese programming.
* Includes English Digimon cards and English manual.

Specifications

  • Size: Package Size: L 5.90" x W 4.33" x H D 1.89"
  • Materials: Device: MABS・PC・SI; Charging Cable: TPE・LCP・PBT
  • Target Age: 15+

Set Contents

  • Digivice: 1
  • Dejica (Digimon Card - English): 1
  • Charging cable: 1
  • Instruction manual: 1

Battery

Includes 1 lithium-ion polymer battery + 1 USB Type-C cable
